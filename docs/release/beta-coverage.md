# Native beta coverage — 27 October 2026

This is an initial staff beta, not full Thorbis-TanStack parity. The source reference is commit 448c238a11245d525d42cc542ce5fe9a512415ff. Native changes started at 811faf60fdcc929618292341c5081e84d65421db.

## Implemented native surfaces

- Better Auth sign-in, restore, workspace selection, capability/feature hydration, expiry and sign-out; all workspace state is invalidated when authority changes. Login and public estimate screens use the authentic Thorbis mark-and-wordmark lockup; signed-in workspace selectors use the verified Thorbis shield mark.
- Schedule and assigned-job filters; a local-day My Day list sorted by scheduled time with server-gated “On my way” and optional ETA; jobs; a Paper-aligned create-job composer for customer, title, and notes that opens job details after creation; notes, manual arrival/progress, pause/resume, scheduling and assignment, closeout requirements and office follow-ups. Customer service-address IDs are kept separate from the organization's branch-scope `job.locationId`.
- Customer search, details and creation, Recent/All/Archived scopes with an independent state filter, and the paginated customer-history timeline. Paper-style contacts support create/edit, primary promotion, and confirmed removal with role, phone, and optional email. Service locations support create/edit/remove, address and notes, service/billing type, map pins, and access/parking details.
- Estimates: list, detail, create draft, revision-bound send/approve/decline, and replacement-draft revisions for sent/declined records with editable line items and up to eight Price Book-aware option packages. Option packages require `estimate_option_signing`; existing packages stay protected when the feature is unavailable, and negative credit lines require `estimate.discount`. Invoices: list/detail, draft line editing, gated Price Book selection, reasoned Price Book overrides, revision-safe save/issue, protected native PDF previews, payment history, manual recording/reconciliation of already-collected payments, and reasoned full refunds with explicit recovery for pending or uncertain outcomes.
- Customer estimate decisions: a token-scoped native screen reviews the issued paper, selects an option, records typed-name consent, and supports approve/decline/request-changes with stale-link recovery. It uses the authentic Thorbis mark and wordmark lockup. HTTPS links are not yet associated with the app, so customers can enter through the `thorbis://document/<token>` scheme only until the AASA host and Apple Associated Domains capability are fixed.
- Job media: selected photos/PDFs up to 8 MiB, signed upload/confirmation and protected download/preview.
- Staff notices, revenue report with incomplete-total disclosure, and paginated Price Book with separately gated detail.
- Device-local fleet, equipment, agreements and team records. Inbox drafts and queued items remain session-local and never imply delivery.
- iPhone now keeps Jobs, Arrive, and Inbox as stable native tabs. Jobs opens on the full jobs list; Arrive reuses My Day and on-the-way actions; Inbox shows only the gated server-backed staff-notice feed. Schedule, customers, message drafts, and Price Book remain under More. The iPad schedule sidebar/list/detail layout is preserved. The audited app-authored controls on these surfaces use 44 pt minimum hit frames; the visible Paper icon shapes stay compact. See [native UI evidence](../design/native-ui-evidence-2026-10-27.md).

## Outstanding parity

- Customer HTTPS Universal Link activation for estimate decisions; the live AASA endpoint redirects into the web SPA and currently returns HTML. Change orders still have only a backend command primitive and no completed TanStack UI to mirror. Receipt workflows and hosted checkout remain open. Invoice terms, due dates, tax totals, and customer delivery remain unavailable in the current invoice API.
- Remaining reports, exports, settings/admin, workflow and broader dispatch features.
- Canonical offline replication and command replay. Production readiness currently reports PowerSync not configured; a client cache is not a substitute.
- Separate Network/member and broader customer-portal flows beyond estimate decisions. Their identities and contracts were discovered; they are not implemented in this staff binary. Portal and Brain packages contain scaffolds rather than completed upstream functionality.
- Camera capture, large multipart media, richer accessibility and authenticated device workflow testing.
