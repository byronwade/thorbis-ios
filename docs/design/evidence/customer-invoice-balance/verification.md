# Customer invoice balance wording

The customer invoice book uses the backend invoice status (`draft`, `issued`, `paid`, `void`) and exact minor-unit amounts to distinguish a draft total, an amount due, no balance due and a reconciled paid invoice. “Paid in full” requires a fresh paid invoice with positive recorded aggregate payments and a complete, identity-checked payment history that reconciles to that aggregate. When history is unavailable, its existing warning remains; stale status and balance are labeled as last known. Existing discount, payment, refund, and total rows remain available. No settlement or payment action changed.

Before the change, `/private/tmp/invoice-balance-red.log` and `.xcresult` record expected UI failures for a draft headline and a zero-dollar invoice headline. A later run exposed an erroneous test expectation for a Paid $0 row; the existing presentation intentionally omits that row when no payment exists, so the test now checks Amount due $0 instead. The final exact-source `/private/tmp/invoice-balance-final-tests.log` and `.xcresult` show 3 amount-summary, 25 job-document and 9 release-note unit tests, plus 4 customer invoice money UI tests passing on iPhone SE (iOS 18.2). These cover draft, zero-dollar, partial payment, fully paid deposit, unavailable history, discount/refund details and job-document paging. The retained screenshot shows the existing refund details; no separate screenshot of the new headline was retained.

The local DEBUG fixtures introduce `--paper-preview-job-invoice-zero`, `--paper-preview-job-invoice-draft`, `--paper-preview-deposit-history-failure` and the literal `Sample payment history unavailable`; release auditing must verify these are absent from the executable. No real payment was attempted. Live reconciliation, physical-device appearance, iPad, VoiceOver, RTL and largest text were not tested in this batch.
