Open a job and choose Show to customer. Browse all of that job's estimates and invoices with swipes, Previous/Next or All documents. Returning from review or signing should keep your place.

Check each invoice's discounts and credits, total, paid amount and remaining balance. Expand Payments and refunds to see each payment's method and date, plus refunds and the amount received after a refund. Switch invoices and confirm the details belong to that invoice.

Review an estimate's current work and price before signing. Paid invoices can still be signed when needed; existing signer information remains visible and paid invoices do not offer another payment. Payment hand-back requires the technician to unlock.

Try large text and dark mode. If a document needs refreshing, restore the connection and use Refresh documents without leaving its page. Other jobs' documents should never appear.

Tap to Pay remains disabled pending distribution qualification. Existing secure payment links and received-payment methods remain available.
