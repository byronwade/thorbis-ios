## Batch 1 (this branch)

Items 1–12 and the radius fixes they touch. Files: `Theme.swift` (weight/size floor, radius, chip and commit
tokens, `inkCommit()`, `PressStyle`, `RowPressStyle`, `SkeletonRows`), `App.swift` (`ErrorBanner`),
`PaperComponents.swift`, `PaperWorkViews.swift`, `PaperJobDetailView.swift`, `PaperCustomerViews.swift`,
`Screens.swift`, `LoginView.swift`, `ServiceLocationSheet.swift`, `MediaView.swift`, `ScheduleMapView.swift`,
`InvoicePaymentCollection.swift`, `WorkspaceViews.swift`. Tests: `AppearanceTests` covers ink contrast
(7:1 in every appearance) and the two-weight/12pt floor.

`PaperHeader` is untouched in batch 1 so it does not collide with the side menu PR; batch 2 takes it.

## Batch 2

Items 13–17: side menu motion, modality, anatomy and header grammar, on top of #15 and #17.

## Batch 3

Items 18–20 and 22: two-line job rows with customer, time and number; row press fill; long-press menus
on job and customer rows; Schedule header without the chip band; share text in words.

## Batch 4

Item 23 and the remaining sheet radii. New customer, contact and service location sheets: Return moves to
the next field and the last field saves; the keyboard type and autofill hints match each field; Cancel is a
word in the sheet bar and the one Save sits at the bottom as the ink commit; inputs are 16 so iOS never
zooms; fields use the control radius with a foreground focus ring. A swipe can no longer throw away typed
work in New job, New customer or the pause sheet. Sheets use the 14 card radius.

## Batch 5

Secondary screens. Loading on Estimates, Invoices, Billing, Team, Reports, Follow-ups, Assign, Notices, Payments,
Media and the job's estimates is skeleton rows, not a spinner (MA-13). Corner radii come from tokens across staff
screens: cards 14, controls and notice wells 12, status badges capsules (MA-10). The job note send circle is ink
(MA-08). Pushed-screen titles are 24/500 on the type ramp (MA-09).
