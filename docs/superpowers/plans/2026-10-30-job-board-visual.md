# Job board visual cues

Technicians scan the job board for the work and its state. Preserve assigned/all/recent filters, search, ordering, status filters, grouping, creation, offline/error states, row navigation and context actions. Reuse the schedule's status marker, pair it with an explicit status word and real customer/date/reference, and let long content reflow. Never fabricate a job photo or location. Confirm the status is exposed with its job row and the row opens, then inspect dark/light at a small phone size. Large text, VoiceOver, iPad, RTL and live data remain separately qualified.
