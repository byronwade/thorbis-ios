# Job payment route identity

A technician can tap the next job action, the Money footer, or return from customer view to collect payment. Capture the requested job-scoped invoice and its loaded payment model as one item-based sheet route; never derive the sheet invoice from a later mutable selection. Refuse the transition if the requested ID, current selection, scoped invoice, loaded model ID or model invoice disagree, or if collection is not currently allowed. Recheck current session, workspace, job and capability after asynchronous preparation. Keep the existing cash/check offline decision in the model. Returning from the sheet continues refreshing invoices and closeout. On failure, leave the technician on Money so the current state can be reviewed.

Regression evidence: missing and switched selection, delayed response racing another selected invoice, foreign job scope, stable sheet ownership, current payment state, customer-handoff route, existing cash/check offline and equal-balance invoice selection. No claim of cross-process denial/cached invoice privacy is made by this navigation fix. Review the actual sheet binding and all collection doors independently before release.
