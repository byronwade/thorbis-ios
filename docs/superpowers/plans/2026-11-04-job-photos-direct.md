# Direct job photos

A technician viewing Money needs one tap to see this job's photos and capture tools. Add Photos as a fourth job section using the existing Activity/Photos state and media view; do not create another data source, change authorization, or remove the Activity filter. Choosing Activity from Photos returns to the all-activity view. Existing photo, note, capture, estimate, invoice and payment entry points stay intact. At accessibility sizes the native section chooser offers Photos by name.

Verify on a mounted phone that Money → Photos uses one section control tap, the photo filter is selected, and Activity and Money remain reachable. Verify the large-text chooser and existing Activity-filter route. Check iPad, narrow phone, selection/VoiceOver labels and 44pt targets; document any unavailable checks. No payment flow or offline storage is changed.
