# Supervised customer photo handoff — prototype (not qualified)

The isolated prototype adds a selected-photo handoff from the staff job media selection bar. It validates an explicit selection of up to 50 currently loaded stored photos against a fresh job read and fresh exact-record reads, then presents one image at a time in the existing protected customer container. Previous/next and swipe are available. It rechecks each exact record before and after a fresh signed-URL download, removes its protected preview file after use, and clears its in-memory image on background or scope invalidation. The endpoint's signed URL is a bearer credential until it expires; a post-download check does not revoke bytes or eliminate the final server/client race.

Status: **not built or released**. Source parsing and two standalone pure Swift harnesses passed for selection and preparation. The initial missing-symbol compilation was not a behavioral red; XCTest and mounted UI tests have not run. The validator, owner, UI, API changes, protected-handback lifecycle, late download cleanup, negative authorization and background cases require independent review and simulator qualification. Exact deployed endpoint contract and online behavior are unverified. Customer selection is read-only; existing staff capture, tagging, archive, share and offline pending flows are intended to remain unchanged but have not been regression-tested with this diff. It is not a remote share or offline customer gallery. Before release, validate signed URL and temp-file cleanup in cancellation/failure paths, real authorization and org/job scope including away-and-back, and visible/recoverable loading or denial states; capture phone/iPad and accessibility evidence.
