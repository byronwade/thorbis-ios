# Schedule visit visuals

A field technician needs to distinguish the job under way from the later visits without reading a wall of text. Keep the Paper PFO schedule's time lane, next-visit band and grouped day list. The existing status-derived symbol and semantic surface give each visit a visual anchor; status remains in words, the next action and job navigation remain unchanged, and longer titles may occupy two lines in the compact list. No address, photo, time or job type is inferred or invented. At accessibility sizes the lead visit's time and marker reflow above the text.

Entry points and retained behavior: Today, Week and Unscheduled retain their current grouping, navigation links and long-press actions; Map is unchanged. The hero still uses the authoritative job transition and pending status. Existing refresh, search, empty, error and offline behaviors are unchanged.

Reference: saved `docs/design/paper-PFO-0.json` (Schedule: next visit and later visits). Paper Desktop was unavailable for a fresh read. The status marker is a deliberate adaptation to the user's request for more meaningful visuals; it uses existing Theme semantic colors and SF Symbols. Simulator screenshots and build results must be recorded before delivery; this file does not certify full-app design parity, authenticated data or accessibility.
