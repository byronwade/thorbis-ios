# Store listing draft

- **Name:** Thorbis
- **Subtitle:** Field service, in your pocket
- **Category:** Business
- **Bundle id:** com.thorbis.field
- **Pricing:** Free. No in-app purchases. Thorbis is sold to companies outside the app; the app never
  shows plans, prices, upgrades or checkout links.

## Description

Thorbis is the field app for service companies that run on Thorbis. See today's route, start driving
and mark arrival, pause and resume work with a reason, and complete the job with notes and photos. Look
up a customer's contacts, site access notes and history, call or text them, or get directions. Send
estimates, issue invoices and see what's been paid. Your company manages accounts and access.

## Review notes

Thorbis requires an account created by a company administrator. Provide a demo account in a test
workspace with a scheduled job assigned to the reviewer. Account deletion is under Account → Delete account.
The camera is used only when the user taps "Take photo" on a job.
